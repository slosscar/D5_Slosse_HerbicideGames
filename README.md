**How to run the touchpoint**
1. Open `arena_bus.blend` in Blender 5.2, or open a new file, go to the *Scripting* tab, open `arena_bus.py` and press *Run Script*.
2. Press Space in the 3D view to play the 40-second animation. The camera switches between the five shots on its own (timeline markers).
3. *Render › Render Animation* writes the MP4 to `Videos/HerbicideGames`.
