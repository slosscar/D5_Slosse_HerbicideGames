## 1_Touchpoint · The Arena Bus (core touchpoint)

| File | What it is | Channel | Spec |
|---|---|---|---|
| `arena_bus.blend` | The Blender file of the Arena Bus: street, bus, two gladiators, screens, speakers, 5 camera shots | Streets of Rome · core touchpoint | Blender 5.2 |
| `arena_bus.py` | Python script that builds the same scene from scratch | Streets of Rome · core touchpoint | Blender 4.2 or newer |
| `stills/shot_1_street.png` | Shot 1: the bus turns into the street | Streets of Rome · core touchpoint | 1600 × 900 px, PNG |
| `stills/shot_2_look_up.png` | Shot 2: the fight on the roof | Streets of Rome · core touchpoint | 1600 × 900 px, PNG |
| `stills/shot_3_screens.png` | Shot 3: side screen with date and place | Streets of Rome · core touchpoint | 1600 × 900 px, PNG |
| `stills/shot_4_speaker.png` | Shot 4: horn speakers on the roof | Streets of Rome · core touchpoint | 1600 × 900 px, PNG |
| `stills/shot_5_drive_away.png` | Shot 5: the bus drives on | Streets of Rome · core touchpoint | 1600 × 900 px, PNG |

**How to run the touchpoint**
1. Open `arena_bus.blend` in Blender 5.2, or open a new file, go to the *Scripting* tab, open `arena_bus.py` and press *Run Script*.
2. Press Space in the 3D view to play the 40-second animation. The camera switches between the five shots on its own (timeline markers).
3. *Render › Render Animation* writes the MP4 to `Videos/HerbicideGames`.
